# Environmental Hazards Module

**Purpose:** Convert environmental dangers and traps from any D&D edition to Shadowdark mechanics.

## Falling Damage

### Standard Formula
**1d6 damage per 10 feet fallen**
- **Maximum:** 20d6 (200 feet)
- **Save:** DC 15 DEX to reduce damage by half (if soft landing possible)
- **Fatal Heights:** 50+ feet potentially lethal for 1st level characters

### Surface Modifiers
- **Soft (hay, water):** Reduce damage by half
- **Spikes/rocks:** Add +1d6 damage
- **Very soft (deep snow):** Reduce damage by 75%

## Fire & Temperature Damage

### Fire Damage by Intensity
| Hazard Type | Damage | Save DC |
|-------------|--------|---------|
| Torch flame | 1d4 | DEX 12 |
| Campfire | 1d6 | DEX 12 |
| Bonfire | 2d6 | DEX 15 |
| House fire | 3d6 | DEX 15 |
| Lava/Inferno | 4d6 | DEX 18 |

### Temperature Extremes
- **Extreme Cold:** 1d6 damage per hour, CON save DC 12 to avoid
- **Extreme Heat:** 1d6 damage per hour, CON save DC 15 to avoid
- **Frostbite/Heatstroke:** Disadvantage on physical checks until treated

## Suffocation & Drowning

### Breath Holding
- **Duration:** CON modifier + 1 minutes (minimum 1 minute)
- **After Limit:** DC 12 CON save each round
- **Failure:** Take 1d6 damage and fall unconscious
- **Death:** Die when reaching 0 HP while unconscious

### Swimming
- **Calm Water:** No check needed
- **Rough Water:** DC 12 STR check each round
- **Stormy Seas:** DC 15 STR check each round
- **Failure:** Begin drowning sequence

## Trap Mechanics

### Detection DCs
| Trap Complexity | Detection DC | Example |
|----------------|--------------|---------|
| Simple/Obvious | 9 | Loose floor stone |
| Standard | 12 | Hidden pressure plate |
| Well-Hidden | 15 | Concealed needle |
| Masterwork | 18 | Nearly invisible wire |

### Disarm DCs
| Trap Type | Disarm DC | Failure Effect |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| Mechanical | 12 | Triggers trap |
| Complex Mechanical | 15 | Triggers trap |
| Magical | 18 | Triggers trap + magical mishap |

### Common Trap Effects

#### Pit Traps
- **10 foot pit:** 1d6 damage, prone condition
- **20 foot pit:** 2d6 damage, prone condition
- **Spiked pit:** Add +1d4 piercing damage
- **Covered pit:** DC 12 WIS to notice before stepping

#### Projectile Traps
- **Dart:** 1d4 damage, possible poison
- **Arrow:** 1d6 damage
- **Crossbow bolt:** 1d8 damage
- **Multiple projectiles:** Roll for each

#### Poison Traps
- **Needle:** 1 damage + poison save
- **Gas:** No damage, CON save or poisoned condition
- **Blade coating:** Normal weapon damage + poison save

## Poison Effects

### Poison Save DCs
| Poison Strength | Save DC | Effect |
|----------------|---------|---------|
| Weak | 12 | Disadvantage on checks for 1 hour |
| Moderate | 15 | 1d6 damage + disadvantage for 8 hours |
| Strong | 18 | 2d6 damage + disadvantage for 24 hours |
| Lethal | 20 | 4d6 damage + paralysis for 1d4 hours |

### Poison Types
- **Injury:** Takes effect when weapon hits
- **Contact:** Takes effect when touched
- **Inhaled:** Takes effect when breathed
- **Ingested:** Takes effect 1d4 rounds after consumption

## Magical Hazards

### Magical Trap Effects
- **Glyph of Warding:** 3d6 damage, DC 15 DEX save for half
- **Symbol:** Various effects, DC 18 WIS save to resist
- **Teleporter Trap:** Randomly teleport 1d100 feet in random direction
- **Polymorph Trap:** DC 18 WIS save or become harmless animal for 1 hour

### Unstable Magic Areas
- **Wild Magic Zone:** Roll on mishap table for any spell cast
- **Dead Magic Zone:** No spells function
- **Enhanced Magic Zone:** All spells cast at +1 tier effectiveness

## Quick Reference Tables

### Common Hazard DCs
- **Slippery surface:** DC 12 DEX or fall prone
- **Unstable footing:** DC 15 DEX or fall
- **Climb slick wall:** DC 18 STR
- **Balance on beam:** DC 12 DEX
- **Force stuck door:** DC 15 STR

### Environmental Damage
- **Acid splash:** 1d6 damage per round until washed off
- **Lightning strike:** 4d6 damage, DC 18 DEX save for half
- **Avalanche/rockslide:** 4d6 damage, DC 15 DEX save for half
- **Quicksand:** Grappled, STR check DC 15 to escape each round
