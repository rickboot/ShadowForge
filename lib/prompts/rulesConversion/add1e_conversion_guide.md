# AD&D 1st Edition to Shadowdark Conversion

**Compatibility: 80% - Moderate conversion needed**

AD&D 1E is more complex than B/X but retains old-school philosophy. Requires simplification.

## Character Classes & Races

### Race + Class to Ancestry + Class
- **All AD&D races** → Use closest Shadowdark ancestry
- **Class Conversions:**
  - Fighter → Fighter
  - Cleric → Priest
  - Magic-User/Illusionist → Wizard
  - Thief → Thief
  - **Eliminate:** Assassin, Monk, Bard (too complex)
  - **Paladin/Ranger:** Use Fighter with appropriate ancestry

## Armor Class & THAC0

### THAC0 to Attack Bonus
**Formula:** Attack Bonus = 20 - THAC0

| THAC0 | Attack Bonus | Typical Level |
|-------|--------------|---------------|
| 20 | +0 | 1st level Fighter |
| 19 | +1 | 2nd level Fighter |
| 17 | +3 | 4th level Fighter |
| 15 | +5 | 6th level Fighter |
| 10 | +10 | 11th level Fighter |

### Descending to Ascending AC
**Formula:** Shadowdark AC = 20 - AD&D AC

| AD&D AC | Shadowdark AC |
|---------|---------------|
| 10 | 10 |
| 5 | 15 |
| 0 | 20 |
| -2 | 22 |
| -5 | 25 |

## Spells

### Spell Level Conversion
- **Levels 1-5:** Direct conversion to Tiers 1-5
- **Levels 6-7:** Convert to Tier 4
- **Levels 8-9:** Convert to Tier 5
- **Simplify complex spell descriptions**

### Spell Save DCs
Use universal conversion table:
- **1st-2nd level spells:** DC 12
- **3rd-4th level spells:** DC 14  
- **5th+ level spells:** DC 15

## Combat Systems

### Eliminate Complex Mechanics
- **Weapon Speed:** Ignore, use standard initiative
- **Weapon vs. Armor Type:** Ignore, use base damage
- **Multiple Attacks:** Max 2 attacks in Shadowdark
- **Surprise:** Use advantage/disadvantage system

## Saving Throws

### Five Saves to Three Saves
| AD&D Save | Shadowdark Save | Ability |
|-----------|-----------------|---------|
| Paralyzation/Poison/Death | Death | STR |
| Rod/Staff/Wand | Spells | WIS |
| Petrification/Polymorph | Spells | WIS |
| Breath Weapon | Death | DEX |
| Spell | Spells | WIS |

## Monsters

### Stat Block Conversion
- **Hit Dice:** Use directly (e.g., "5+2 HD" = Level 5, 5d8+10 HP)
- **Armor Class:** Convert using AC formula above
- **Attacks:** Keep damage, ignore complex attack routines
- **Special Abilities:** Simplify to 1-2 sentences maximum
- **Magic Resistance:** Convert to advantage on spell saves

## Examples

### AD&D Orc Conversion
**AD&D:** AC 6, 1 HD, THAC0 19, 1d8 damage
**Shadowdark:** Level 1, AC 14, HP 4, +1 attack, 1d8 damage

### AD&D Troll Conversion
**AD&D:** AC 4, 6+6 HD, THAC0 13, 1d4+1/1d4+1/1d8+4 damage
**Shadowdark:** Level 6, AC 16, HP 30, +6 attack, 2 claws 1d6 each, regeneration
