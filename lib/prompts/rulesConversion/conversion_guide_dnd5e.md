# D&D 5th Edition to Shadowdark Conversion

**Compatibility: 70% - Moderate conversion needed**

5E's modern design requires adjustment to old-school sensibilities.

## Character Classes

### Subclasses to Base Classes
- **All Fighter subclasses** → Fighter
- **All Cleric domains** → Priest
- **All Wizard schools** → Wizard  
- **All Rogue archetypes** → Thief
- **Other classes:** Convert to closest equivalent or eliminate

## Monster Conversion Adjustments

### Power Level Rebalancing
- **Reduce HP by 25-30%** (5E characters have more HP than Shadowdark)
- **Reduce attack bonuses by 2-4** (5E uses higher bonuses due to proficiency)
- **Keep damage dice the same** or reduce by one size if too deadly
- **Legendary Actions:** Remove or convert to extra attacks
- **Lair Actions:** Convert to environmental hazards

## Combat Mechanics

### Simplify Complex Systems
- **Action Economy:** 1 action + movement only
- **Bonus Actions:** Incorporate into main action or eliminate
- **Reactions:** Keep only opportunity attacks
- **Death Saves:** Use Shadowdark's dying timer instead

## Spell Conversion

### Spell Levels to Tiers
- **Cantrips:** Eliminate or convert to Tier 1
- **Levels 1-2:** Convert to Tiers 1-2 directly
- **Levels 3-4:** Convert to Tiers 2-3
- **Levels 5-6:** Convert to Tiers 3-4
- **Levels 7-9:** Convert to Tiers 4-5

### Spell Mechanics
- **Concentration:** Becomes Focus spells
- **Ritual Casting:** Eliminate
- **Spell Slots:** Replace with Shadowdark failure system

## Status Effects

### 5E Conditions to Shadowdark
| 5E Condition | Shadowdark Effect |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Frightened | Disadvantage on attacks |
| Charmed | Must obey simple commands |
| Poisoned | Disadvantage on checks |
| Paralyzed | Cannot act |
| Stunned | Cannot act |
| Restrained | Cannot move |
| Prone | Disadvantage on attacks |
| Blinded | Disadvantage on sight-based tasks |

## Proficiency & Skills

### Eliminate Complex Systems
- **Proficiency Bonus:** Ignore
- **Skills:** Convert to ability checks only
- **Advantage/Disadvantage:** Keep system directly
- **Expertise:** Convert to advantage on specific checks

## Examples

### 5E Goblin Conversion
**5E:** AC 15, 7 HP, +4 to hit, 1d6+2 damage
**Shadowdark:** Level 1, AC 15, HP 5, +1 attack, 1d6 damage

### 5E Owlbear Conversion
**5E:** AC 13, 59 HP, +7 to hit, 2d8+5/1d10+5 damage
**Shadowdark:** Level 4, AC 13, HP 20, +4 attack, 2 attacks 1d8 each

**Note:** Significantly reduced HP to match Shadowdark's lower character HP totals.
