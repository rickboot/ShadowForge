# Status Effects & Conditions Module

**Purpose:** Convert complex conditions from any D&D edition to simple Shadowdark effects.

## Standard Condition Conversions

| Source Condition | Shadowdark Effect |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Paralyzed/Stunned/Incapacitated | Cannot act |
| Frightened/Shaken | Disadvantage on attacks |
| Charmed | Must obey simple commands |
| Poisoned/Sickened | Disadvantage on checks |
| Blinded | Disadvantage on attacks and sight-based checks |
| Deafened | Disadvantage on hearing-based checks |
| Prone | Disadvantage on attacks until standing |
| Grappled/Restrained | Cannot move |
| Exhausted (1-2 levels) | Disadvantage on physical checks |
| Exhausted (3+ levels) | Disadvantage on all checks |
| Unconscious | Cannot act, advantage on attacks against |

## Duration Conversions

| Source Duration | Shadowdark Duration |
|----------------|---------------------|
| 1 round | 1 round |
| 1 minute | 10 rounds |
| 10 minutes | 1 turn |
| 1 hour | 1 hour |
| 8 hours | Until next rest |
| 24 hours | 1 day |
| Permanent | Until removed |
| Save ends | Save each round to end |
| Concentration | Focus (maintain each round) |

## Disease & Poison Effects

### Poison Template
- **Onset:** Immediate or 1d4 rounds
- **Effect:** Disadvantage on specific checks/attacks
- **Save:** DC 12 CON to resist initial effect
- **Recovery:** DC 12 CON save after each rest to recover
- **Duration:** Until cured or successful save

### Disease Template
- **Incubation:** 1d4 days
- **Effect:** Disadvantage on physical checks, -1 to stat per day
- **Save:** DC 15 CON to resist progression
- **Recovery:** Two consecutive successful saves to recover
- **Death:** If any stat reaches 0

## Specific Condition Rules

### Sleep Effects
- **Duration:** 1d4 rounds or until damaged
- **Wake Conditions:** Damage, loud noise, physical contact
- **Immunity:** Undead, creatures above Level 2

### Charm Effects  
- **Effect:** Must obey reasonable commands from caster
- **Limitations:** Cannot be commanded to obviously harm self
- **Save:** New save if commanded to act against nature
- **Duration:** Typically 1d8 hours

### Fear Effects
- **Effect:** Disadvantage on attacks, must move away if possible
- **Save:** WIS save to resist, can retry each round
- **Immunity:** Undead, creatures without intelligence

### Paralysis Effects
- **Effect:** Cannot move or act, attacks against have advantage
- **Save:** STR save to break free (physical paralysis) or WIS save (mental)
- **Duration:** Until save succeeds or effect expires

## Environmental Conditions

### Temperature Effects
- **Extreme Cold:** 1d6 damage per hour, CON save DC 12 to avoid
- **Extreme Heat:** 1d6 damage per hour, CON save DC 15 to avoid
- **Hypothermia:** Disadvantage on all checks until warmed

### Suffocation
- **Breath Holding:** CON modifier + 1 minutes (minimum 1)
- **After Limit:** DC 12 CON save each round
- **Failure:** 1d6 damage and unconscious
- **Death:** At 0 HP while suffocating

### Intoxication
- **Light:** Disadvantage on DEX-based checks
- **Moderate:** Disadvantage on all checks
- **Heavy:** Cannot act coherently, treat as confused

## Quick Reference

**Most Common Conversions:**
- Paralyzed → Cannot act
- Frightened → Disadvantage on attacks  
- Charmed → Obey commands
- Poisoned → Disadvantage on checks
- Stunned → Cannot act
- Prone → Disadvantage until standing
