# Shadowdark RPG - Complete Rules

## What is Shadowdark RPG?

Shadowdark RPG is a tabletop role-playing game where one person (the Game Master or GM) describes a dangerous fantasy world, and other players control fictional characters (adventurers) who explore that world. Players roll dice to determine if their actions succeed or fail.

## Core Game Concept

Players are adventurers who explore dark, dangerous places (dungeons, ruins, caves) seeking treasure and glory. The game emphasizes:
- Constant time pressure from limited light sources
- Deadly combat where characters can die permanently
- Dangerous magic that can backfire catastrophically
- Resource management where every torch, spell, and hit point matters

## What Makes Shadowdark Unique

- **Real-time torch burning:** Light sources burn for actual real-world time
- **Spell failure consequences:** Magic spells can fail spectacularly
- **High lethality:** Characters die quickly, making every decision crucial
- **Exploration focus:** Designed around dungeon crawling and survival

## Basic Game Mechanics

### Dice and Checks
- **Primary die:** 20-sided die (d20) for most important rolls
- **Other dice:** 4-sided (d4), 6-sided (d6), 8-sided (d8), 10-sided (d10), 12-sided (d12)
- **Dice notation:** "1d6" means roll one 6-sided die, "2d8" means roll two 8-sided dice and add them
- **Core mechanic:** Roll d20 + modifier vs Difficulty Class (DC)
  - If result ≥ DC: Success
  - If result < DC: Failure

### Character Stats

Every character has six core abilities (stats) rated 3-18:

| Stat Score | Modifier | Stat Score | Modifier |
|------------|----------|------------|----------|
| 1-3 | -4 | 12-13 | +1 |
| 4-5 | -3 | 14-15 | +2 |
| 6-7 | -2 | 16-17 | +3 |
| 8-9 | -1 | 18+ | +4 |
| 10-11 | 0 | | |

**The Six Stats:**
- **Strength (STR):** Physical power, melee fighting, lifting heavy objects
- **Dexterity (DEX):** Agility, ranged combat, stealth, quick reflexes
- **Constitution (CON):** Health, endurance, resisting poison/disease
- **Intelligence (INT):** Reasoning, knowledge, wizard spellcasting
- **Wisdom (WIS):** Perception, intuition, priest spellcasting
- **Charisma (CHA):** Personality, leadership, social influence

### Difficulty Classes (DCs)
- **Easy (DC 9):** Simple tasks with some risk
- **Normal (DC 12):** Standard challenging tasks
- **Hard (DC 15):** Difficult tasks requiring skill
- **Extreme (DC 18):** Nearly impossible feats

### Advantage and Disadvantage
- **Advantage:** Roll d20 twice, use the better result
- **Disadvantage:** Roll d20 twice, use the worse result
- **Cancellation:** If you have both, they cancel out and you roll normally

### Critical Results
- **Natural 20 (critical success):** Always succeeds spectacularly
- **Natural 1 (critical failure):** Always fails with potential complications

### Luck Tokens
- **Awarded by GM:** For exceptional roleplay, heroism, or clever actions
- **Limit:** Each player can only have one luck token at a time
- **Use:** Spend to reroll any d20 roll you just made, must use new result
- **Sharing:** Can give your luck token to another player

## Character Classes

### Fighter - The Warrior
**Equipment:**
- **Weapons:** Can use ALL weapons
- **Armor:** Can wear ALL armor types and use shields
- **Hit Points:** Roll 1d8 + Constitution modifier per level

**Special Abilities:**
- **Weapon Mastery:** Choose weapon type, get +1 attack/damage plus half level
- **Grit:** Choose STR or DEX, get advantage on checks to overcome opposing forces
- **Hauler:** Add CON modifier to carrying capacity

### Priest - The Divine Caster
**Equipment:**
- **Weapons:** Club, crossbow, dagger, mace, longsword, staff, warhammer
- **Armor:** Can wear ALL armor types and use shields
- **Hit Points:** Roll 1d6 + Constitution modifier per level

**Magic:**
- **Spellcasting Stat:** Wisdom
- **Spell Source:** Divine power from chosen deity
- **Special:** Automatically knows "Turn Undead" spell
- **Spell Progression:** Starts with 2 tier-1 spells, gains more by level

### Thief - The Specialist
**Equipment:**
- **Weapons:** Club, crossbow, dagger, shortbow, shortsword
- **Armor:** Leather armor only
- **Hit Points:** Roll 1d4 + Constitution modifier per level

**Special Abilities:**
- **Backstab:** Extra weapon dice damage vs unaware enemies (1 + half level)
- **Thievery:** Advantage on climbing, stealth, hiding, traps, locks, delicate tasks

### Wizard - The Arcane Caster
**Equipment:**
- **Weapons:** Dagger, staff only
- **Armor:** Cannot wear ANY armor
- **Hit Points:** Roll 1d4 + Constitution modifier per level

**Magic:**
- **Spellcasting Stat:** Intelligence
- **Spell Source:** Arcane study
- **Learning:** Can learn spells from scrolls (DC 15 INT check, scroll consumed)
- **Spell Progression:** Starts with 3 tier-1 spells, gains more by level

## The Magic System

### Spellcasting Process
1. Declare spell and target
2. Roll spellcasting check: d20 + relevant stat modifier
3. Compare to DC: 10 + spell tier
4. Apply results

### Spellcasting Check Results
- **Success (≥ DC):** Spell works as described
- **Failure (< DC):** Spell fails, cannot cast that spell again until rest
- **Critical Success (natural 20):** Spell works + may double one numerical effect
- **Critical Failure (natural 1):** Spell fails + mishap/penance + cannot cast until rest

### Spell Tiers and DCs
| Tier | DC | Power Level |
|------|-----|-------------|
| 1 | 11 | Basic magic |
| 2 | 12 | Moderate magic |
| 3 | 13 | Powerful magic |
| 4 | 14 | Very powerful magic |
| 5 | 15 | Legendary magic |

### Focus Spells
- **Duration:** "Focus" - requires ongoing concentration
- **Maintaining:** Make spellcasting check at start of each turn
- **Success:** Spell continues until start of next turn
- **Failure:** Spell ends
- **Interruption:** Taking damage requires immediate focus check
- **Limitation:** Can only focus on one spell at a time

### Wizard Mishaps (Natural 1)
Roll on mishap table based on spell tier:
- **Explosion:** Take damage
- **Refraction:** Target yourself instead
- **Gear loss:** Random equipment disappears
- **Status effects:** Glow, scream, disadvantage on future spells
- **Spell loss:** Cannot cast specific spells for extended time
- **Reality distortion:** Open gates, petrification, etc. (higher tiers)

### Priest Penance (Natural 1)
- Cannot cast failed spell until penance + rest
- **Penance options:**
  - Material sacrifice (spell tier × 5 gold pieces value)
  - Holy quest for deity
  - Ritualistic atonement
- Inadequate penance makes spell loss permanent

## Combat System

### Combat Flow
1. **Initiative:** d20 + Dexterity modifier, highest first, continue clockwise
2. **Each turn:** 1 action + move near distance (30 feet)
3. Continue until resolved

### Attack Resolution
- **Melee Attack:** d20 + Strength modifier vs target's AC
- **Ranged Attack:** d20 + Dexterity modifier vs target's AC
- **Success:** Roll weapon damage, subtract from target's HP

### Armor Class (AC)
- **Base:** 10 + Dexterity modifier
- **Leather Armor:** AC = 11 + Dex modifier
- **Chainmail:** AC = 13 + Dex modifier
- **Plate Mail:** AC = 15
- **Shield:** +2 AC

### Death and Dying
- **0 Hit Points:** Character falls unconscious and is dying
- **Death Timer:** Roll 1d4 + Constitution modifier (minimum 1)
- **Dies in that many rounds** unless helped
- **Each turn while dying:** Roll d20, natural 20 = rise with 1 HP
- **Stabilization:** DC 15 Intelligence check by another character
- **Death:** Permanent character removal

### Critical Hits
- **Natural 20:** Automatically hits + double damage dice

## Time, Light, and Exploration

### Light Sources
| Source | Duration | Range |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Torch | 1 real hour | Near (30 feet) |
| Lantern | 1 hour per oil | Double near (60 feet) |
| Campfire | 8 hours | Near (30 feet) |

### Darkness Rules
- **Total Darkness:** Disadvantage on sight-based tasks
- **Random encounters:** Check every round in total darkness
- **Most monsters:** Dark-adapted (ignore darkness penalties)

### Exploration
- **Turn structure:** GM describes → Players act → GM resolves
- **Random encounters:** Based on area danger level
- **Real-time tracking:** Torches burn during actual play

### Random Encounter Frequency
- **Deadly areas:** Every round
- **Risky areas:** Every 2 rounds
- **Unsafe areas:** Every 3 rounds
- Roll d6, encounter on 1

### Resting
- **Requirements:** 8 hours sleep + 1 ration consumed
- **Benefits:** Recover all HP, regain all spells
- **Interruption:** Each disturbance requires DC 12 CON check or rest fails
- **Dangerous areas:** Random encounter checks during rest

## Monster Guidelines

### Basic Statistics
- **Level (LV):** Overall power (0-20+)
- **Armor Class (AC):** Defense rating
- **Hit Points (HP):** Level × d8 + CON modifier (minimum 1)
- **Attacks:** Number per turn
- **Stats:** Same six stats as characters

### Monster Levels
- **0-3:** Weak creatures
- **4-6:** Dangerous creatures
- **7-9:** Formidable creatures
- **10+:** Legendary creatures

### Special Properties
- **Dark-Adapted:** Most non-humanoids ignore darkness penalties
- **Undead:** Immune to morale, don't flee
- **Greater Undead:** Only damaged by silver or magic
- **Impervious:** Only damaged by specific sources

### Morale
- Each monster has a Morale score
- **Check when:** Reduced to half numbers/HP, surprised, or leader defeated
- **Roll:** d20 + Wisdom modifier vs DC 15
- **Failure:** Monster flees or surrenders
- **Undead:** Immune to morale checks

## Essential Spells

### Tier 1 Priest Spells
- **Cure Wounds:** Touch heals (1 + half level)d6 HP
- **Light:** Object glows 1 hour real time, near distance
- **Holy Weapon:** Weapon becomes magical +1 for 5 rounds
- **Turn Undead:** Undead in near area flee or destroyed
- **Protection from Evil:** Chaotic beings get disadvantage, focus spell
- **Shield of Faith:** +2 AC for 5 rounds

### Tier 1 Wizard Spells
- **Magic Missile:** Advantage on casting, 1d4 damage at far range
- **Sleep:** Creatures level 2+ in near area fall asleep
- **Burning Hands:** 1d6 damage to all in close area
- **Light:** Same as priest version
- **Mage Armor:** AC becomes 14 for 10 rounds
- **Charm Person:** Humanoid level 2+ becomes friend for 1d8 days

### Tier 2 Examples
- **Hold Person (Wizard):** Focus, paralyze humanoid level 4+
- **Invisibility (Wizard):** Target invisible 10 rounds or until attacking
- **Bless (Priest):** Give target a luck token
- **Zone of Truth (Priest):** Focus, target cannot lie

### Tier 3 Examples
- **Fireball (Wizard):** 4d6 damage in near cube at far range
- **Animate Dead (Wizard):** Corpse becomes loyal zombie for 1 day
- **Mass Cure (Priest):** All allies in near regain 2d6 HP
- **Dispel Magic (Wizard):** End one spell effect

## Character Ancestries

### Human
- **Ambitious:** Extra talent roll at 1st level
- **Linguistic:** Know Common + one additional language

### Elf
- **Farsight:** +1 ranged attacks OR +1 spellcasting checks
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Sylvan

### Dwarf
- **Stout:** +2 HP, advantage on HP rolls per level
- **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish

### Halfling
- **Stealthy:** Invisible 1/day for 3 rounds
- **Languages:** Common

### Half-Orc
- **Mighty:** +1 melee attack and damage
- **Languages:** Common, Orcish

### Goblin
- **Keen Senses:** Cannot be surprised
- **Languages:** Common, Goblin
