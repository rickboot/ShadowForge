# Shadowdark RPG Quick Reference

**Core Mechanic:** Roll d20 + modifier ≥ Difficulty Class (DC)

## Difficulty Classes
- **Easy:** DC 9
- **Normal:** DC 12  
- **Hard:** DC 15
- **Extreme:** DC 18

## Character Classes
- **Fighter:** d8 HP, all weapons/armor, weapon mastery
- **Priest:** d6 HP, divine spells (WIS), Turn Undead
- **Thief:** d4 HP, backstab, thievery advantages
- **Wizard:** d4 HP, arcane spells (INT), no armor

## Combat Basics
- **Armor Class:** 10 + armor bonus + DEX modifier
- **Initiative:** d20 + DEX modifier, highest first
- **Death:** 0 HP = dying, roll 1d4+CON rounds until death
- **Critical Hit:** Natural 20 = double damage dice

## Magic System
- **Spell Tiers:** 1-5 (replacing spell levels)
- **Casting Check:** d20 + stat modifier ≥ (10 + spell tier)
- **Failure:** Cannot cast that spell until rest
- **Critical Failure (1):** Mishap + spell loss until rest

## Ancestries
- **Human:** Extra talent roll, bonus language
- **Elf:** +1 ranged attacks OR +1 spellcasting, farsight
- **Dwarf:** +2 HP, advantage on HP rolls per level
- **Halfling:** Invisible 1/day for 3 rounds
- **Half-Orc:** +1 melee attack and damage
- **Goblin:** Cannot be surprised

## Time & Light
- **Torches:** Burn 1 real hour
- **Movement:** Near = 30 feet, Far = 60 feet
- **Rest:** 8 hours sleep + 1 ration = recover all HP/spells

## Saves
- **Death (STR-based):** Avoid death, poison, paralysis
- **Spells (WIS-based):** Resist magic effects
- **Luck Token:** Reroll any d20, must use new result
